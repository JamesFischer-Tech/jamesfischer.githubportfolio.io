About Me

I was born and raised in the greater seattle area. I have been surrounded by technology me entire life, during my childhood I discovered that I had a love for gaming, this love for gaming is what formed my initial interest in computing. In 2021 I dived further into my passion by creating a YouTube channel which turned into a full-scale online buisness. I networked through platforms like discord and telegram with other likeminded individuals and developed and sold online products for video games, I would promote these products via YouTube channel which was linked to my website where viewers could purchase my products. Early on projects like these led me to discover the endless opportunites in the realm of computing which futher motivated me to pursure computer science as well as buisness in my studies.

My college education began when I enrolled in the running start program through Bellevue College during my junior and senior years of highschool, this experience granted me vantage in my futuer studies as I had finished pre-req courses that I would have needed to take later on, in addition I got a pastiche of the college experience early on in my life. My freshman year of college was spent at MSU in Bozeman, Montana. I then transferred to UW my sophmore year where I am now completing my studies! I have goals for app development and game development, I plan to pursure game development once I am finished with my first mobile app project.
