About Me

I was born and raised in the greater seattle area. I have been fascinated & surrounded by computers and technology my entire life. During my childhood I discovered that I had a love for gaming, this love for gaming is what formed my initial interest in computing. In 2021 I dived further into my passion by creating a YouTube channel which turned into a full-scale online buisness. I networked through platforms like discord and telegram with other likeminded individuals and developed and sold online products for video games, I would promote these products via my YouTube channel which had links to my website where viewers could purchase my product. Early on projects like this is what led me to discover the seemingly endless opportunites in the realm of computing which futher motivated me to pursure computer science as well as buisness in my studies at University.

My college education began when I enrolled in the running start program through Bellevue College during my junior and senior years of highschool, this experience granted me vantage in my futuer studies as I had finished pre-req courses that I would have needed to take later on, in addition I got a pastiche of the college experience early on. My freshman year of college was spent at MSU in Bozeman, Montana. I then transferred to UW my sophmore year where I am now completing my studies! I have goals for app development and game development, I plan to pursure game development once I am finished with my first mobile app project which I hope to get started on once I profit from my current project!
