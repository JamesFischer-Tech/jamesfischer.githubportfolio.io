About Me

I was born and raised in the greater Seattle area. I have been fascinated & surrounded by computers and technology my entire life. During my childhood, I discovered that I had a love for gaming; this love for gaming is what formed my initial interest in computing. In 2021, I dived further into my passion by creating a YouTube channel, which turned into a full-scale online business. I networked through platforms like Discord and Telegram with other like-minded individuals and developed and sold online products for video games. I would promote these products via my YouTube channel, which had links to my website where viewers could purchase my products. Early on, projects like this were what led me to discover the seemingly endless opportunities in the realm of computing, which further motivated me to pursue computer science as well as business in my studies at University.

My college education began when I enrolled in the Running Start program through Bellevue College during my junior and senior years of high school; this experience granted me a vantage in my future studies as I had finished pre-req courses that I would have needed to take later on. In addition, I got a taste of the college experience early on. My freshman year of college was spent at MSU in Bozeman, Montana. I then transferred to the University of Washington my sophomore year, where I am now completing my studies! I have goals for app development and game development. I plan to pursue game development once I am finished with my first mobile app project, which I hope to get started on once I profit from my current project!
