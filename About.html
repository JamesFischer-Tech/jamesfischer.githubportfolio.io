About Me

I was born and raised in the greater seattle area. I have been surrounded by technology me entire life, during my childhood I discovered that I had a love for gaming, this love for gaming is what formed my initial interest in computing. In 2021 I dived further into my passion by creating a YouTube channel which turned into a full-scale online buisness. I networked through platforms like discord and telegram with other likeminded individuals and developed and sold hacks for video games, I would promote these products on my YouTube channel which had a link to my website where could purchase these products. Computing is such an endless tool, a tool that any creative person can use and have a blast with, my endless creativity is what motivates me and further spurs me in this subject, as I want and plan to do so many different things in the realm of computing, from developing hacks, to soon applicatiosn on IOS store, to then game development.

My college education began when I enrolled in the running start program through Bellevue College during my junior and senior years of highschool, this experience granted me vantage in my futuer studies as I had finished pre-req courses that I would have needed to take later on, in addition I got a pastiche of the college experience early on in my life. My freshman year of college was spent at MSU in Bozeman, Montana. I then transferred to UW my sophmore year where I am now completing my studies!
