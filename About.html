About Me

I was born and raised in the greater seattle area. I have been surrounded by technology me entire life, during my childhood I discovered that I had a love for gaming, this love for gaming is what formed my initial interest in computing. In 2021 I dived further into my passion by creating a YouTube channel which turned into a full-scale online buisness. I networked through platforms like discord and telegram with other likeminded individuals to develop and sell hacks for video games, I would then promote the hacks on my YouTube channel which had links to my website where I would sell the product outright

My college education began when I enrolled in the running start program through Bellevue College during my junior and senior years of highschool, this experience granted me vantage in my futuer studies as I had finished pre-req courses that I would have needed to take later on, in addition I got a pastiche of the college experience early on in my life.
